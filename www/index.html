    Privacy

    YOUR INFO

    Privacy policy

    Staten Island Snow Removal (a snow-removal service of Empire State Fence, Staten Island, NY) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect in this app and how we use it.

    What we collect

    When you join the winter contact list we ask for your name, street address, email, phone, preferred contact method, service type (driveway / sidewalk / both / commercial), and any optional notes you add. If you opt into storm alerts, that preference is stored on your device (localStorage) so we know you want notices.

    How we use it

    We use your information only to contact you about snow removal service, storm updates, and related winter work on Staten Island. We do not sell your information or share it for unrelated marketing.

    Who gets it

    Signup details are sent to our business inbox so our local crew can follow up. Service providers that host our email or app backend may process data solely to deliver that service. We do not use your contact info for third-party advertising.

    On-device storage

    Storm-alert preference, saved property, local request stubs, winter stamp progress, and (if a submit fails offline) a local copy of your signup may stay on this device in browser / app storage. Clearing app data or site data removes it.

    Kids

    This app is for adults arranging property service. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

    Contact / requests

    Questions, updates, or a request to remove your winter-list info: email statenislandsnowremovals@gmail.com or call / text 718-981-5151. Staten Island, NY.
